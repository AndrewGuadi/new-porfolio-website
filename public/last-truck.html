Rules and scoring

Choose a ticket or tap its marker to preview the shortest road route. Confirm with Drive. Each road segment uses one fuel and one minute. The kitchen refills both meal slots on arrival. Fuel cannot be refilled. A late delivery still counts. Serve all four customers to finish. The service window stays open while you think.

Each delivery earns 100 points. Deliver by the printed minute for 40 extra. At the end, each unit of fuel saved earns 5 more. Best scores are saved on this device when browser storage is available. Tonight’s market changes with the local date.
