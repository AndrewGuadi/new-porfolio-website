How I became Hampden 11’s minority inspector of elections

I worked the polls in Cumberland County before I ran for office. In 2025, somebody told me an independent couldn’t win. I’m an independent. I wanted to serve the precinct where I live, so I ran a small write-in campaign.

I won a four-year term as Minority Inspector of Elections for Hampden Township’s 11th precinct. If you’ve ever thought about working at the polls, the county has a page that explains what the day involves and how to put your name in.

What I bring to the festival

I’m part of the team behind Wish Upon a Food Truck in Hershey. The festival brings local food trucks, families, volunteers, and sponsors together to benefit Make-A-Wish Philadelphia, Delaware & Susquehanna Valley.

One place I can help is the website. A food truck owner needs to know what it takes to bring a truck. A sponsor needs to see where their business fits. A family needs a date, a time, and a place to go. Those are three different jobs for the same page.

Time with Toastmasters

I also spent time with the Effective Communicators Toastmasters club. Practicing a speech in a room full of people is a different kind of work from sitting down to write code. In both places, I care whether the person on the other end can follow what I’m trying to say.
