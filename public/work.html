George’s Oasis · Lehigh Valley

How many are coming? How many are playing?

George’s Oasis has a restaurant, an ice cream counter and a mini golf course. A group of twenty can have twelve golfers and eight people who just want lunch. One head count doesn't tell the restaurant enough to help.

On the group outings page, we ask for both numbers. Families can see how a meal and a round fit together. An organizer can find out what happens to lunch if it rains. For takeout and delivery, I kept the ordering services the restaurant already used. The parts of the business that worked deserved to come along with the redesign.

Wish Upon a Food Truck · Hershey

A food truck application with a person at the other end.

I’m part of the team behind Wish Upon a Food Truck, a festival that supports Make-A-Wish. My shop builds and maintains the website. One decision we made for 2027 was what a food truck owner should have to do on the very first visit.

We ask whether they’re bringing a truck or a trailer, because that affects the setup. They can leave the menu, logo and insurance certificate for later. Then we tell them who will follow up. Her name is Jodi. The form does enough work to give two people a useful place to start their conversation.

Teaching Jobs in PA · Education

One search across district lines.

A Pennsylvania teacher can be qualified for the same job in several neighboring districts. Each district posts vacancies its own way. I wrote software to collect those postings and bring them into one directory.

Try looking up biology in the 2025 edition. You can narrow the list by location and follow a result back to the district that posted it. I wanted the computer to do the repetitive part of the search and leave the teacher with the information to decide where to apply.
